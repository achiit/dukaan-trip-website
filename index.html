A look inside our campus.

A self-guided look at where our students spend their days — the library, the labs, the field, the studio, and the hallway in between.

The route

Five stops around campus

First stop

The Library

Twelve thousand books, a reading loft under the skylight, and a standing rule: if you can find a quieter corner, it's yours for the year.

Open 7:30am – 5:30pm
Second stop

The Science Labs

Three labs share one long bench of sinks and burners. Students grow crystals here in week one; by the final year, some run their own research projects.

Room 114–116
Third stop

The Playing Field

Cricket and athletics take turns on this field, with a scoreboard that has clocked more matches than anyone can count.

Home to school sports teams
Fourth stop

The Art Studio

North-facing windows, paint-splattered floors, and a hallway gallery that rotates every term.

Open studio hours after 3pm
Last stop

The Main Hallway

Every stop connects back here. Lockers on one side, class photos on the other, and the loudest four minutes of every school day.

Passing period: 4 minutes

See it in person

Tours run weekday mornings and by appointment. Bring your questions — we'll bring the tea.
